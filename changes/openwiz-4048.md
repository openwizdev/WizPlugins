WizBar's board core gets a Grill view (`board-core/views/grill-picker.ts`): grill questions one at a time, the recommended option first and marked, checkboxes when a question takes several answers, a notes field, an "other" field and Next. It is keyboard-first and fits a phone. Each answer is checked against its question card and becomes one line for the Wiz thread in the `openwiz.grill-picker.v1` and decision answer v1 framing, kept exactly as given.
